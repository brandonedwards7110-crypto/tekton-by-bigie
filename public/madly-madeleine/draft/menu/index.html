Madeleine questions

All about madeleines

What is a madeleine?

A madeleine is a small French cake baked in its traditional shell-shaped mold. It is soft and buttery inside, lightly golden around the edges and recognized by the small hump that forms during baking.

Is a madeleine a cake or a cookie?

It's a cake, not a cookie. A madeleine is a small French cake baked in a traditional shell-shaped mold.

Why the shell shape?

The traditional mold gives each madeleine its signature shape and lightly golden edges.

What about the little hump?

A well-baked madeleine rises in the center, creating its characteristic hump and soft interior.

How do you enjoy a madeleine?

With coffee, with tea or as a special break during your day.
