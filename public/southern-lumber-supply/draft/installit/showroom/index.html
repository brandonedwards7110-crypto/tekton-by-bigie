Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Meet with our sales staff at your home or here in the showroom, and we will help you plan the project to finish on time and within budget.

Do you install what you sell?

Yes. You pick it out, and if you like, our experienced installers take care of the installation.

Is financing available?

Financing is available upon request. Ask us when you stop in or call.

Who do you work with?

Homeowners and commercial businesses, across the Wiregrass area and the Florida Panhandle.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
